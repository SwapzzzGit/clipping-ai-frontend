Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Clipper Studio ("we", "the app") turns long videos into short clips and publishes the clips you approve to the social accounts you connect. The app is operated by the Clipper Studio team.

What we collect

  • Connected account details. When you connect an Instagram professional account through Meta, we receive the account's ID, username, display name and profile picture URL, and the list of accounts you chose to share.
  • Access tokens. We store the access token that lets us publish to the accounts you connected. Tokens are encrypted on our server, and we use them only to do what you ask, such as publishing a clip.
  • Your content. The video links or files you submit, the clips we render, and the transcripts we create from them.
  • Activity. The jobs you run, their status, the posts we published, and the links to those posts.

How we use it

  • To find moments in a video worth clipping, and to write titles and captions.
  • To publish the clips you approve to the accounts you choose, and to show you the status and link of each post.
  • To keep the service running and fix problems.

We do not sell your data. We do not use data from Instagram or Meta for advertising.

Who processes your data

We use these providers to run the app:

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), for sign-in and publishing.
  • Google, for access to YouTube videos and, depending on configuration, Gemini models that choose clips and write captions.
  • Anthropic, if the configuration uses Claude for these tasks.
  • Render (backend hosting) and Vercel (website hosting).

Each provider processes data under its own terms.

How long we keep it

We keep a connected account's tokens until you disconnect the account. We keep clips, transcripts and job records until you delete them or ask us to delete them.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any account on the Accounts & categories page.
  • Delete a job from its job page.
  • Ask us to delete your data, as described on the data deletion page.

Children

Clipper Studio is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, and we will change the date at the top.
