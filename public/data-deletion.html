Data deletion instructions

Last updated: 1 November 2026

You can delete the data Clipper Studio holds about you, and remove our access to your Instagram account, in the three steps below.

1. Disconnect the account in Clipper Studio

  1. Sign in to Clipper Studio and open Accounts & categories.
  2. Under Connected accounts, press Disconnect and confirm.

This deletes the access token we stored for that account, along with the account record in our app. It does not remove Clipper Studio's access from your Meta account. Do step 2 for that.

2. Remove Clipper Studio's access in Meta

In your Meta account settings, open the business integrations or app permissions for your Instagram or Facebook account, and remove Clipper Studio.

3. Ask us to delete everything else

Email broadwayswap98@gmail.com from the address you use with us. Use the subject Data deletion and include the Instagram username you connected. We will delete your connected account records, access tokens, jobs, clips and transcripts within 30 days, and we will confirm by email.

What we keep

Deleted records are removed from our live systems. Copies held in our hosting providers' logs may remain for a limited time.
