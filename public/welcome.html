How it works

Three steps, and you stay in charge of what goes out.

  1. Add a video

    Paste a YouTube link. We read the captions and download only the parts you clip, not the whole video.

  2. Review the clips

    Each moment gets a score and a reason. Keep the ones you like, and change the start or end of any clip.

  3. Publish or schedule

    Post now, pick a time, or use the next free slot on each account. Every post shows its link once it is live.

Built for the way creators post

Each clip is cut, captioned and written for the place it will appear.

Moments with reasons

We read the transcript and pick moments with a strong hook, with a short reason you can check before you approve.

Formats for every place

Render 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, or 4:5, 1:1, 2:3 and 16:9 for other places. Captions are sized for each format.

A caption for each platform

Each platform gets its own title and caption, written within its length and hashtag limits.

Connect once

Sign in to your account through the platform's own login. Clipper Studio never sees your password, and you can disconnect at any time.

A calendar you can move

See scheduled and published posts by month or week, and drag a post that has not gone out yet to a new day.

Rules you set

Approve each clip yourself, or set a category to publish its strongest clips automatically. Scheduled posts go out at the time you chose.
